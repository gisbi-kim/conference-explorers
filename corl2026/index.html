Counting method · Sources · Interpretation

For 2024 and 2025, we counted paper entries in the official PMLR main-conference proceedings. For 2026, we counted unique papers in the official CoRL programme as of 15 October 2026. Workshop papers are excluded. Proceedings counts may differ from initial acceptance decisions; the 2026 figure is not a final proceedings count.

Growth = (687 ÷ previous-year public paper count − 1) × 100. The 2026 figures of 2,094 active submissions and a 32.8% acceptance rate come from the official announcement. Submission definitions have not been harmonized across years, so we do not compare submission growth or acceptance-rate trends here. The increase in paper count is documented; changes in research quality or the growth of individual topics require separate analysis.

Memory, context and transfer in generalist policies; world models coupled with control; and reliable data, evaluation, contact and whole-body execution are prominent themes. This interpretation draws on official sessions and paper content, not year-over-year topic growth.

SOURCES & LIMITATIONS

How to read this explorer

Programme titles, presentation types, days and session themes follow the official CoRL programme. When an external title differs, it is shown separately. Authors and abstracts for all 687 papers, plus 686 PDF links were refreshed from public OpenReview records on 18 October 2026. Affiliations and other links retain their separately cited sources. Each paper detail includes sources and affiliation evidence.

Institution aliases are normalized only from names found in paper affiliations or bylines. A paper with multiple institutions counts once for each, so totals may exceed the number of papers. Authors with identical names may be merged by name string. Bookmarks are stored only in this browser.
