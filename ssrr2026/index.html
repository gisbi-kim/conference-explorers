SOURCES & LIMITATIONS

Sources and interpretation

This dataset structures the SSRR 2026 accepted-papers programme supplied by the site owner on 16 October 2026. Paper IDs, titles, author spellings, sessions and start times are preserved. Venue and dates were checked against the official website. The programme may change.

The 46 papers are unique entries in the supplied list. Submission totals, acceptance rates and historical trends were not provided. Abstracts, affiliations and verified PDF or code links are also unavailable in this dataset. External search links do not establish that a matching paper or full text is available.

Topic tags use regular-expression matches against English titles. Tags overlap and are not an exhaustive semantic classification. Relevant papers without matching title keywords may be missed. Topic comparisons use denominators of 46 papers overall and six award finalists. All EDA is computed from the same JSON dataset.
